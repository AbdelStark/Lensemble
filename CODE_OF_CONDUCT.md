# Code of Conduct

Lensemble is a research community. Participation requires respectful,
evidence-oriented collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors and research subjects with respect.
- Critique claims, methods, and artifacts rather than people.
- State uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and evidence limitations plainly.
- Respect data residency, privacy, licensing, and responsible-disclosure
  boundaries.
- Make space for differing backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
misrepresentation, doxxing, and publication of another person's private
information are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
project events, and private project communication. Maintainers may edit or
remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

To report conduct privately, ask the repository owner through a profile-listed
contact channel for a confidential conduct-reporting address. Do not put
sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be handled as privately as
practical, with conflicts of interest routed away from the subject of the
report.

Good-faith disagreement, replication failure, and correction of a public claim
are not conduct violations.
